How to Seed a Lawn: From Soil Preparation to First Mowing

A step-by-step guide to preparing soil, sowing grass seed, watering, germination, first mowing and repairing bare areas.

Step-by-step process

1

Prepare the site

Remove debris, roots, old turf and perennial weeds. Resolve obvious drainage issues before adding the final seedbed.

2

Loosen and level the soil

Break compaction, create a workable root zone and level the surface carefully.

3

Firm the seedbed

Lightly roll or firm the surface so it is stable but not compacted like concrete.

4

Choose the mixture

Match species to use, shade, water availability and maintenance expectations.

5

Sow evenly

Split seed into two equal portions and sow in crossing directions.

6

Lightly rake and press

Improve seed-to-soil contact without burying the seed deeply.

7

Keep the surface moist

During germination, prevent the upper seedbed from drying out while avoiding flooding.

8

First mowing

When grass is firmly rooted and roughly 8–10 cm high, use a sharp blade and remove only the upper third.

9

Repair bare patches

Loosen sparse areas, overseed, press the seed in and maintain moisture.

The biggest early-stage risks are poor seed-to-soil contact, drying during germination, washing seed into piles and mowing too low too soon.
