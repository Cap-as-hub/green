Project overview

Good preparation makes the biggest difference.

Lawn establishment is a sequence: prepare the ground, choose the right seed, keep moisture consistent, and avoid stressing young grass too early.

🌱

Prepare the soil

Remove debris and weeds, loosen compacted soil, level the surface and create a fine seedbed.

💧

Keep it evenly moist

New seed needs frequent, light watering. The top layer should stay moist without becoming waterlogged.

✂️

Mow at the right time

Wait until the grass is firmly rooted and tall enough before the first cut. Never remove too much at once.

Step by step

The lawn-growing process

Use this sequence as a simple project plan for a new lawn or a complete renovation of an old one.

01

Clear and shape the area

Remove stones, roots, construction debris and persistent weeds. Correct major dips and bumps before adding the final soil layer.

02

Prepare the topsoil

Loosen the upper soil layer, rake it level and lightly firm it. The surface should be even, but not compacted like concrete.

03

Sow the grass seed

Spread seed evenly in two crossing directions. Lightly rake or press the seed into the surface so it has good soil contact.

04

Water consistently

Use a fine spray and keep the seedbed consistently moist during germination. Avoid strong streams that can wash seed into piles.

05

First mow and early care

Make the first cut only when the grass is established. Use sharp blades and gradually move toward the normal mowing height.

Small details, better results.

Most lawn problems begin with uneven soil, inconsistent moisture, poor seed-to-soil contact or mowing too early.

✓ Water gently — do not wash seed away.
✓ Keep foot traffic off young grass.
✓ Use sharp mower blades for the first cut.
✓ Fill thin areas early before weeds take over.
