Project overview

Build your lawn step by step.

A successful lawn project usually follows the same logic: prepare the area, choose the right seed mix, sow evenly, keep the surface moist, repair thin spots, and mow only when the grass is ready.

🌱

Start with the soil

Remove debris, weeds and stones, level the ground and create a fine seedbed with good seed-to-soil contact.

💧

Water consistently

Freshly seeded ground should stay evenly moist. Light, frequent watering is better than strong washing streams.

✂️

Manage early growth

Reseed empty patches when needed and wait for the right moment before the first mowing.

Choosing the right mix

Grass types and seed mix purposes

Different seed mixes are designed for different needs: quick repair, slow growth, family use, shade tolerance or heavy traffic.

Quick Germination Mix

  • Fast early coverage
  • Good for quick results
  • Useful for patch repair

Slow-Growing Mix

  • Lower mowing frequency
  • Often more decorative
  • Suitable for low-maintenance lawns

Family / Play Lawn

  • Balanced appearance and durability
  • Good for home gardens
  • Handles regular walking

Sports / High-Traffic Mix

  • Strong wear tolerance
  • Best for active use
  • Suitable for play areas and sport-style lawns

Shade-Tolerant Mix

  • Better for less sunny areas
  • Useful near fences and trees
  • Often slower to establish
Year-round care

Seasonal Lawn Calendar

This simple seasonal overview helps you understand what to focus on during each part of the year.

Spring

  • Clean debris and inspect winter damage
  • Rake lightly and repair bare spots
  • Apply spring fertilizer if needed
  • Start regular mowing as growth increases

Summer

  • Water deeply and consistently
  • Mow regularly, but do not cut too short
  • Watch for stress, yellowing and dry areas
  • Limit heavy work during extreme heat

Autumn

  • Best time for overseeding and repair
  • Fertilize to strengthen roots
  • Remove falling leaves
  • Prepare lawn for colder months

Winter

  • Avoid unnecessary traffic on frozen grass
  • Do not work wet or frozen soil
  • Plan seed type and spring repairs
  • Service mower and tools for the next season

How to upload and replace the photos

This website is already prepared to load photos from the images folder. If you later want to replace the current pictures with your own, follow these steps:

  1. Open your GitHub repository green.
  2. Upload the new files into the images folder.
  3. Keep the same file names, or change the file names in index.html.
  4. Commit the changes in GitHub.
  5. Vercel will automatically redeploy the updated website.
