Build Your Lawn

Choose the lawn use, light, soil and watering possibilities. A good mixture should match the site, not just the product name.

  • Family lawns often benefit from a balanced ryegrass + fescue + bluegrass mix.
  • Heavy-traffic lawns need better wear tolerance and recovery.
  • Dry, sunny areas may benefit from tall fescue and drought-tolerant species.
  • Shade requires more fescues and realistic wear expectations.

Grass Types

Perennial ryegrass

Fast establishment, high wear tolerance.

Kentucky bluegrass

Dense turf and strong recovery through rhizomes.

Red fescue

Fine leaf texture and useful shade tolerance.

Tall fescue

Deep-rooted and more tolerant of drought and heat.

Fine fescues

Lower-input, slower-growing options for lighter use or shade.

Problem solver

  • Brown grass can be caused by drought, mowing too short, fertilizer burn or root-zone problems.
  • Yellow grass can point to drainage, nutrient imbalance, compaction or weak roots.
  • Bare patches often need both diagnosis and overseeding, not seed alone.
  • Moss usually indicates weak turf and unsuitable site conditions.

Seasonal lawn calendar

For Lithuania and Northern Europe, timing depends more on soil temperature and weather than on fixed dates. Spring focuses on wake-up and repair; early autumn is often the best renovation period.
