Lawn Fertilizer Guide: N-P-K and Seasonal Feeding

Understand what nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium do, when to fertilize a cool-season lawn and when feeding can make stress worse.

What N-P-K means

N — Nitrogen

Drives leaf growth and green colour. Too much can create soft growth, more mowing and higher disease risk.

P — Phosphorus

Supports root development, especially during establishment. Mature lawns often need less unless soil testing shows a need.

K — Potassium

Supports stress tolerance, water regulation and winter hardiness. It is often emphasized in autumn lawn products.

More is not better. Apply the label rate evenly and avoid overlapping spreader passes. Do not use strong nitrogen fertilizer as a universal response to yellow or brown grass.
